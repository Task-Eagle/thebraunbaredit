Virtual String Studio

This premium virtual instrument simulator enables realistic chord playing, individual string picking, and beautiful strumming. Use a pair of headphones for optimal acoustic frequency details!

Interaction Modes

  • Chord Pad Mode: Tap on quick-chord buttons (like C, F, G7, Am) on the left. The app instantly fingers the instrument's frets so that strums yield harmonious chord soundscapes.
  • Fretboard Mode: Gain complete manual control! Touch arbitrary frets on the virtual instrument fretboard on the left to set custom hand positions.

How to Play Sound

  • Strumming: Hold and drag your cursor/finger continuously across the visual strings on the right.
  • Individual picking: Simply tap any single string directly.
  • Keyboard Shortcuts: Use numerical keys 1 to 6 to quickly simulate rolling strums.
